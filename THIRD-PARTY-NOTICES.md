## Matt Pocock's skills

The questioning method in `SKILL.md` is adapted from Matt Pocock's skills,
inspected at commit `c55ee46073ed923f86ce59a5eb3b6d895095d1b7`:

- [grill-me](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/c55ee46073ed923f86ce59a5eb3b6d895095d1b7/skills/productivity/grill-me/SKILL.md)
- [grilling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/c55ee46073ed923f86ce59a5eb3b6d895095d1b7/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md)
- [Upstream license](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/c55ee46073ed923f86ce59a5eb3b6d895095d1b7/LICENSE)

The adaptation retains dependency-aware questioning, evidence gathering, and
revising subsequent questions after answers. It replaces the user interview
with typed Jev questions during task execution. Jev transport, state management,
answer interpretation, and execution feedback are additions. It does not import
the upstream skill, require its installation, mandate subagents, or reproduce
its user-confirmation workflow. Recommended answers are omitted to avoid leading
the decision model.
